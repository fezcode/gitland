# Gitland 0.14.4

## Windows release

Download and run **Gitland-Setup-0.14.4.exe**. The Windows x64 installer includes the .NET runtime.

## Changes

### Atelier appearance

- Introduce Atelier's tinted glass styling across the title bar, sidebar, workspaces, menus, and dialogs, while keeping source and merge editors opaque and readable.
- Make Acrylic the default material for new settings and appearance resets. Existing theme, font, and material choices are preserved.
- Customize the material, tint presets, hue, hex color, glassiness, transparency, and color strength in Settings. Changes apply immediately without losing merge drafts.
- Keep addition and deletion colors distinct, provide an opaque fallback when Windows composition is unavailable, and maintain readable button labels even with very light custom tints.
- Refine navigation, spacing, rounded controls, and compact merge layouts. Selected rows respond immediately without overlapping hover highlights.

### Responsive Workspace scans

- Scan repositories in the background so navigation and Settings remain available, including during refresh and rescan.
- Prevent late scans from replacing another view or its status, and prevent older results from overwriting a newer scan.
- Show scan failures in Workspace with a retry option, and safely handle leaving or closing the window during a scan.
